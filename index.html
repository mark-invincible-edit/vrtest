How it works

Move
Hands into the ground or any wall, push away. Let go to fly.
Climb
Same push on pillars and the stepped rocks.
Tag
When your left wrist says IT, touch a bot with your body or hands.
Score
Stay free as long as possible. Streak and best are on your left wrist.
