Knuckle
Tag

Five bots, one of them is it. No legs. Throw your hands into the ground and shove yourself across the arena.

How it works

Move
Hands into the ground or any wall, push away. Let go to fly.
Modes
Left wrist panel. Stick up/down picks, A/X/B/Y starts.
Classic
Tag in the big arena. Stay free as long as you can.
Hunter
You are the killer in a huge maze. Touch every bot before the timer ends.
Killer
Same, but a bigger, faster bot is hunting you. Die and the round restarts with a new killer.

Needs a headset browser (Quest Browser) over https.
